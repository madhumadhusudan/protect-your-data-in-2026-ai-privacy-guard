# AI Privacy Guard — Privacy Policy

## Important Notice
The Privacy Risk Score (PRS) generated by AI Privacy Guard is an **AI-detected privacy exposure estimate** based on detected visual content. It does NOT constitute a legal privacy guarantee.

## Data Retention & Control
- Users hold full ownership of uploaded content.
- Users can click "Delete My Data" or "Clear Local History" anytime to purge cached projects.
- Metadata (EXIF/GPS) stripping is recommended prior to public sharing.
