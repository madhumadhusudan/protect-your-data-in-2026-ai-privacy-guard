# Dataset & Training Documentation

## Datasets Architecture
- `datasets/faces/`: Adult & Child faces with age annotations.
- `datasets/license_plates/`: Indian high-resolution and low-resolution license plates.
- `datasets/documents/`: Synthetic Indian Aadhaar, PAN, Voter ID, Passport samples.
- `datasets/payment_cards/`: Synthetic Credit/Debit card datasets with Luhn valid masks.
- `datasets/qr_codes/`: UPI and Payment QR codes.
- `datasets/text_pii/`: Annotated scene text with phone, email, and residential address tags.

## Indian-Context Dataset Highlights
- Indian road conditions, varied urban/rural lighting, regional signage (English, Hindi, Kannada, Tamil).
- Strict train/validation/test split with zero data leakage.
